# Weekly Founder Update Writer

Package wins, blockers, and asks into a crisp weekly update instead of a scattered note dump.

## What this pack does
Turn raw notes into a founder update for investors, advisors, or your team with a consistent structure.

## Who it is for
- Founders sending investor or advisor updates
- Small teams sharing weekly progress
- Agencies syncing clients internally

## Setup time
About 8 minutes.

## Required inputs
- **Weekly notes**: Metrics, wins, blockers, and raw team notes
- **Audience**: Investors, advisors, internal team, or clients

## Optional inputs
- **Previous update**: Last week's update for continuity and week-over-week narrative
- **Metric screenshots**: Dashboard images or exported charts

## What you get
- Wins, blockers, and asks
- Week-over-week narrative when your notes give last week's figures
- Clear next priorities
- A format stakeholders can scan quickly

## Limitations
- Uses only the notes you paste in; it will not add metrics or wins you did not report.
- Keeps each update to under 300 words, so a full board pack needs a different format.
- It leaves out items that look unsuitable for the stated audience and lists them for you, but you decide what is confidential.
- If your notes state no priorities for next week, it proposes them from your open blockers and asks you to confirm.
- It cannot check your numbers against your dashboards; it reports what the notes say.

## Install / use steps
1. Paste your weekly notes, metrics, or Slack dump.
2. Choose the audience for the update.
3. Review the draft and send without rewriting from scratch.
