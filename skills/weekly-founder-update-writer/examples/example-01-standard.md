# Example: Friday update after a strong product launch week

## Scenario
A seed-stage B2B SaaS founder needs to send a weekly update to 12 angel investors after a busy launch week. Raw notes are scattered across Slack, a dashboard, and a voice memo.

## Input
- **Weekly notes**: "Shipped v2.1 on Tuesday — new onboarding flow live. 4 qualified demos booked (up from 1 last week). MRR: $18.2K (was $16.8K). Activation rate: 38% (down from 42% — need to investigate, might be the new flow). Lost 1 customer ($200/mo, said they're consolidating tools). Blocker: mobile responsive issues on the pricing page, dev is swamped. Need intro to someone who has run marketplace ops. Also exploring a potential partnership with a Zapier competitor."
- **Audience**: "Angel investors — they want traction proof and specific asks."

## Expected output
*Note to the founder, not part of the update: the potential partnership with a Zapier competitor is left out as unsuitable for this audience. Add it back if you want it shared.*

**Headline: MRR up to $18.2K and 4 qualified demos booked, with an activation dip to chase down**

**Wins**
- Shipped v2.1 with the new onboarding flow (Tuesday)
- Booked 4 qualified demos, up from 1 last week
- MRR up to $18.2K from $16.8K (+8.3%)

**Blockers**
- Activation rate dipped to 38% (from 42%) — investigating whether the new onboarding flow is the cause
- Pricing page has mobile responsive issues; dev capacity is the bottleneck

**Churn note**
- Lost 1 account ($200/mo MRR) — customer said they are consolidating tools.

**Asks**
- Intro to someone who has run marketplace ops

**Next week** (proposed from your notes, please confirm)
- Find out whether the new onboarding flow caused the activation drop
- Get the pricing page mobile issues fixed

## Why this is a good result
The update is under 200 words, leads with traction, is honest about the activation dip, and has one clear ask. An investor can scan it in 30 seconds.
