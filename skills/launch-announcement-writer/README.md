# Launch Announcement Writer

Ship the announcement posts the same day you ship the feature, in three formats.

## What this pack does
Turn a product update into launch posts for X, LinkedIn, and email.

## Who it is for
- Founders shipping updates every week or two
- Indie hackers who build faster than they announce
- Small teams without anyone owning launch comms

## Setup time
About 8 minutes.

## Required inputs
- **What shipped**: The update and what it changes for the person using it
- **Audience and CTA**: Who you are announcing to and the one action you want them to take

## Optional inputs
- **Voice reference**: Optional. A past post you liked, so the drafts match your tone.
- **Screenshot or demo link**: The asset each post points at
- **Recent announcements**: What you have already posted, so the launch does not repeat itself

## What you get
- Short X post with a clear hook
- Longer LinkedIn post with context and outcome
- Launch email with subject line options
- One consistent call to action across all three

## Limitations
- Writes from the update details you paste in; it does not read your changelog or check what actually shipped.
- The posts are drafts: you add the link and any screenshot and publish them yourself.
- A bug fix or minor tweak gets a one-line roundup entry, not three posts.
- It matches your voice only as far as the reference post you supply; without one the drafts are plain and flagged as not tone-matched.
- It makes no claims about results, adoption, or time saved beyond what you tell it.

## Install / use steps
1. Describe what shipped and what it changes for the user.
2. Add your audience and the one action you want them to take.
3. Run the pack to get the X, LinkedIn, and email versions.
4. Adjust the voice, add your link, and publish all three the same day.
