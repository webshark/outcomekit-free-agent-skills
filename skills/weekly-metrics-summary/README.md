# Weekly Metrics Summary

Turn raw weekly numbers into a narrative your team can actually use in meetings.

## What this pack does
Summarize weekly metrics, outliers, likely drivers, and recommended follow-up in one operating brief.

## Who it is for
- Founders running weekly business reviews
- Agencies summarizing account health
- Ops leads turning dashboard noise into action

## Setup time
About 8 minutes.

## Required inputs
- **Metric export**: Copied dashboard values, spreadsheet rows, or BI export
- **Primary KPIs**: The few metrics your team actually steers by

## Optional inputs
- **Previous week's summary**: For trend comparison and narrative continuity
- **Alert thresholds**: Custom thresholds for flagging anomalies

## What you get
- Metric deltas and outliers
- Likely drivers to investigate
- Suggested questions for the team
- Reusable weekly review summary

## Limitations
- Uses only the numbers you paste in; it does not pull from your dashboard or fill in missing values.
- Likely drivers are hypotheses to check, not confirmed causes.
- Flags anything that moves more than 15% week over week unless you give your own thresholds, so small counts can look bigger than they are.
- One week of data does not support strategic conclusions, so it suggests questions rather than pivots.
- Needs prior-week values in the export or in last week's summary to show changes.

## Install / use steps
1. Paste the weekly metrics or upload a dashboard export.
2. Add your most important KPIs and alert thresholds.
3. Review the summary before the team sync or founder review.
