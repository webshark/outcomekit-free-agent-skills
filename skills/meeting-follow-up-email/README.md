# Meeting Follow-Up Email Writer

Send the recap while the call is still fresh, with next steps nobody has to interpret.

## What this pack does
Turn call notes into a same-day follow-up email with clear next steps.

## Who it is for
- Founders coming out of back-to-back calls
- Client-facing teams that need a written record
- Anyone who keeps meaning to send the recap tomorrow

## Setup time
About 6 minutes.

## Required inputs
- **Call notes**: Rough notes, bullets, or a transcript from the meeting
- **Recipient and goal**: Who receives the recap and what you want them to do next

## Optional inputs
- **Prior thread**: Earlier emails so the recap continues the thread instead of starting a new one
- **Preferred length**: Short confirmation or detailed recap

## What you get
- Same-day recap email in a scannable format
- Next steps with an owner and a date each
- Open questions written as questions, not assumptions
- A detailed version on request

## Limitations
- Works only from the notes you paste in; it cannot remember what was said on the call.
- Keeps pricing and internal commentary out of the email, so add anything sensitive yourself.
- Dates the notes did not give are proposed, so check them before sending.
- Aims for under 200 words; ask for the detailed version if you need more.
- Notes that record no decisions produce a short note with one proposed next step, not a full recap.

## Install / use steps
1. Paste your call notes, transcript, or rough bullets right after the meeting.
2. Add the recipient and what you want them to do next.
3. Review the draft, confirm the owners and dates, and send it the same day.
