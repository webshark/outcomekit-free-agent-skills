# Meeting Prep Brief

Assemble the context, agenda, and smart questions before an important meeting.

## What this pack does
Create a prep brief with context, goals, risks, and suggested questions before customer, candidate, or internal meetings.

## Who it is for
- Founders juggling too many context switches
- Client-facing teams preparing fast
- Hiring managers or operators entering important conversations

## Setup time
About 6 minutes.

## Required inputs
- **Meeting notes or thread**: Calendar invite, notes, email thread, or CRM context
- **Meeting objective**: The outcome you want from the conversation

## Optional inputs
- **CRM history**: Past deal notes, support tickets, or account health data
- **Attendee profiles**: LinkedIn or internal profiles of other participants

## What you get
- Context snapshot and meeting goal
- Suggested agenda and questions
- Risks or unknowns to validate
- Follow-up reminder structure

## Limitations
- Works only from the notes and history you paste in; it does not look up the company or the attendees.
- Gives you questions and risks to check, not predictions of how the meeting will go.
- Includes a suggested agenda unless you ask for questions only.
- With only a calendar invite you get a short brief built around questions.
- Treat the brief as internal and remove the risk notes before sharing it with attendees.

## Install / use steps
1. Paste notes, calendar context, or thread history.
2. Add the desired outcome.
3. Use the brief right before the meeting or hand it to a teammate.
