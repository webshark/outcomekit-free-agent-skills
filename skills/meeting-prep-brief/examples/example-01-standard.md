# Example: Prep for a customer renewal meeting

## Scenario
A customer success manager is preparing for a renewal conversation with a mid-market account that has been a customer for 11 months. Usage has been inconsistent and there is one open support ticket.

## Input
- **Meeting notes or thread**: "Renewal meeting with Brightpath Logistics, Thursday 2pm. Account: Pro plan, $899/mo, 11 months. Main contact: Lisa Park, Head of Ops. Usage: Active 3 of last 4 weeks. Open ticket #4471: data export formatting issue, submitted 6 days ago, no resolution yet. Last QBR (January): Lisa mentioned they were evaluating whether to expand to the warehouse team (15 additional seats). NPS survey response last month: 7/10, comment: 'Product is good but support response time is frustrating.'"
- **Meeting objective**: "Secure renewal and explore expansion to warehouse team."

## Expected output
**Meeting Prep Brief — Brightpath Logistics Renewal**

**Goal**: Secure the renewal ($899/mo, which is $10,788 over 12 months if the term stays annual) and advance the warehouse team expansion conversation (15 additional seats; pricing is not in the notes).

**Context snapshot**:
- Pro plan customer, 11 months, $899/mo
- Usage: Generally active but dropped off for 1 of the last 4 weeks
- Open support ticket (#4471, data export formatting, 6 days unresolved)
- Last QBR: Lisa expressed interest in expanding to warehouse team
- NPS: 7/10 with a note about support response time

**Suggested agenda**:
1. Acknowledge the open ticket and share a resolution timeline (do this first)
2. Review the value delivered over the past 11 months — bring 2-3 specific usage wins
3. Ask about the warehouse team evaluation — is the need still active?
4. Present renewal terms and expansion pricing if the conversation is positive

**Smart questions**:
- "At the January QBR, you mentioned exploring the warehouse team rollout. Where does that stand?"
- "Beyond the export issue, is there anything else that would make renewal a harder decision?"
- "If we expanded to the warehouse team, who would own the rollout on your side?"

**Risks to validate**:
- The unresolved support ticket is a possible churn risk — expect it to come up, and it may slow the renewal decision
- The NPS comment about support response time may point to a wider pattern; ask rather than assume
- Usage dipping in 1 of 4 weeks could indicate declining engagement
- The notes do not include the renewal date, renewal terms, or expansion pricing. Confirm all three before the meeting.

**Follow-up structure**:
- Send renewal proposal within 24 hours of the meeting
- Resolve ticket #4471 before or immediately after the meeting
- If expansion interest is confirmed, schedule a scoping call with the warehouse team lead

## Why this is a good result
The brief connects the open ticket, NPS feedback, and usage data into a coherent risk picture. The agenda puts the support issue first (which builds trust) and the questions are specific to this account, not generic renewal scripts.
